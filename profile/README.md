## What we build

Careti is a digital loyalty platform for Tunisian businesses. We bring digital stamp cards, rewards, and customer updates to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Digital stamp cards | Keep track of repeat visits with a digital loyalty card. |
| Rewards | Give customers rewards as they return. |
| Customer updates | Share updates through customers' wallet cards. |
| Wallet support | Let customers keep their cards in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. |

## Highlights

- Built for Tunisian businesses and their customers.
- Loyalty cards that customers keep in their phone's wallet.
- Digital stamps and rewards that give every visit a purpose.
